# Third-party notices

This Skill adapts and synthesizes concepts from two MIT-licensed projects:

- [ResearchPilot-Skills](https://github.com/LMDHQ-0420/ResearchPilot-Skills), Copyright (c) 2025 CCFA-Skill contributors.
- [anti-defensive-writing-Skill](https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/anti-defensive-writing-Skill), Copyright (c) 2026 Adkid-Zephyr.

ResearchPilot-inspired concepts include the staged research-to-writing workflow, evidence-first section order, source reconciliation, section/figure planning, claim–evidence review, and reverse outlining. Anti-defensive-writing-inspired concepts include the press-release principle, strongest-value narrative selection, avoidance of project-log and self-undermining prose, experiments as arguments, and narrative reconstruction around valid evidence.
